## Meta Brings Pocket, an App That Lets You Vibe-Code and Share Games, to US Users

![Meta brings Pocket, an app that lets you vibe-code and share games, to US users | TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/pocket-image.png?resize=1200,675)

**Meta** has expanded the availability of its experimental gaming app, **Pocket**, to all users in the U.S. This comes after a quiet launch last month in Brazil, where the app was first tested. **Pocket** empowers users to create small, interactive games using **AI prompts**, which can then be shared and browsed through a scrollable feed.

The app is rooted in **Meta's** acqui-hire of the team behind the **vibe-coded gaming platform Gizmo**. This acquisition, which occurred earlier in **2026**, brought expertise in generative game creation to **Meta's** **Superintelligence Labs**. **Pocket** represents a novel approach to game development, lowering the barrier to entry for creators by leveraging **AI** to translate user ideas into playable experiences.

> **Meta's Pocket** app democratizes game creation by allowing users to generate and share interactive games simply through **AI prompts**, ushering in a new era of 'vibe-coding'.

## Model Release Notes | OpenAI Help Center

![OpenAI](/logo.png)

**OpenAI** has announced several significant updates and retirements to its **GPT** models. Most notably, **GPT-5.6 Sol**, described as their flagship reasoning model for complex work across coding, research, science, cybersecurity, computer use, and design, is now rolling out to eligible paid **ChatGPT** plans. Free, Go, and logged-out users are not included in this initial rollout, and availability may vary.

In May **2026**, **GPT-5.5 Instant** received an update to improve its response style and quality, making it more natural and better paced. Concurrently, **OpenAI** is retiring older models: **OpenAI o3** will be retired from **ChatGPT** on **August 26, 2026**, and **GPT-4.5** on **June 27, 2026**. Previously, in March **2026**, **GPT-5.4 mini** was rolled out to Free and Go users via the "Thinking" feature, and an update to **GPT-5.3 Instant** improved its follow-up tone.

> **OpenAI's** continuous model evolution focuses on advancing capabilities with **GPT-5.6 Sol** for complex tasks while streamlining offerings by retiring older, less-used models to enhance focus on newer, more capable systems.

## AI Governance Needs ISO-like Interoperability Protocols...

Researchers Azmine Toushik Wasi, Mst Rafia Islam, Mahfuz Ahmed Anik, Taki Hasan Rafi, Md Manjurul Ahsan, and Dong-Kyu Chae argue that effective **AI governance** requires a shift from jurisdiction-specific laws to **ISO-like interoperability protocols**. They contend that current approaches, such as the **EU AI Act** and **NIST AI Risk Management Framework**, create a fragmented regulatory landscape that hinders global, standardized risk communication for **AI** systems.

The paper proposes developing standardized **AI nutrition labels** that would include unified metrics for bias, energy usage, and data provenance. These machine-readable manifests would facilitate cross-jurisdictional compliance, reduce redundant efforts for small and medium enterprises (**SMEs**), and build public trust. Drawing inspiration from the **GDPR's** operationalization through standards like **ISO 27001**, the authors advocate for modular, versioned protocols designed to evolve with technological change, promoting a shared global language for responsible **AI** deployment.

> Effective **AI governance** must move beyond fragmented laws towards **ISO-like interoperability protocols** and standardized **AI nutrition labels** to ensure transparent, cross-border risk communication and compliance.
