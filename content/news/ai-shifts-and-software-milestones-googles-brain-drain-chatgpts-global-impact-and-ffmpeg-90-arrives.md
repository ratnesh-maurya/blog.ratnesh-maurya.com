> Despite ongoing hype, current **Generative AI** models like **LLMs** still have inherent limitations, requiring significant human oversight and critical thinking for complex software engineering tasks, suggesting that a fully 'agentic AI' is not 'just around the corner.'

## FFmpeg 9.0 | Hacker News
![FFmpeg 9.0 | Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/s.gif)
The highly anticipated **FFmpeg 9.0** has been officially released, bringing a substantial number of new features and improvements to the open-source multimedia framework. The changelog for this version indicates a strong focus on enhanced video processing capabilities and broader format support.

Key additions in **FFmpeg 9.0** include extended **AMF Color Converter (vf_vpp_amf) HDR** capabilities, **LCEVC** track muxing support in **MP4 muxer**, and the introduction of a **Playdate** video encoder and muxer. Users will also benefit from the new **v360_vulkan** filter, **HE-AAC 960** decoding, and the **transpose_cuda** filter. A particularly welcome feature, as highlighted by users 'harshreality' and 'GlumWoodpecker' on **Hacker News**, is the inclusion of an **Animated WebP decoder** and demuxer, addressing a long-standing gap in desktop application support for this format.

> **FFmpeg 9.0** introduces critical enhancements such as **Animated WebP** decoding, expanded **HDR** support, and new **Vulkan** and **CUDA** filters, significantly boosting its utility for modern multimedia workflows.
