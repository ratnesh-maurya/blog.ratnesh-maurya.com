## Mark Zuckerberg Tells Staff That AI Agents Haven’t Progressed as Quickly as He’d Hoped
![Mark Zuckerberg, CEO of Meta](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GettyImages-1968119319.jpg?w=1024)
**Meta** CEO **Mark Zuckerberg** recently shared at an internal town hall that the development of AI agents has not "accelerated in the way" executives had initially expected. This candid admission follows a significant corporate restructuring earlier this year, where **Meta** laid off approximately **8,000 employees** (around **10 percent** of its corporate workforce) and reassigned another **7,000** to various AI-focused groups, including one named Agent Transformation. 

**Zuckerberg** commented that the job cuts were not as "clean" as they should have been and were driven by concerns about the company's speed in adapting to the evolving tech industry. While acknowledging that the anticipated benefits of the new AI-centric structure haven't "come to fruition yet," he expressed belief that improvements from these AI investments would become apparent within the next **three to six months**. This suggests a recalibration of expectations within **Meta** regarding the immediate impact of AI agents.

> The perceived upside of Meta's new AI-focused company structure "hadn’t come to fruition yet," though improvements are expected in the next three to six months.

## The Browser Wars Aren't About Search Anymore — Here Are the Best Alternatives to Chrome and Safari
![Web search bar on laptop screen 3d render - illustration of website form for research of information on computer](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/search-engine.jpg?w=1024)
The browser wars have entered a new era in **2026**, shifting focus from mere search results to which company's AI can best act on a user's behalf directly within the browser. While **Google Chrome** and **Apple's Safari** continue to dominate the market, largely due to **Chrome's** aggressive integration of generative AI into search, a new wave of challengers is emerging. These alternative browsers, backed by both startups and Big Tech, envision the browser as less of a web window and more of a proactive assistant.

Users now have a growing array of choices beyond the traditional giants. The competitive landscape includes innovative **AI-powered browsers** like **Perplexity's Comet**, which functions as a chatbot-based search engine capable of summarizing emails, browsing web pages, and even performing tasks such as sending calendar invites. Other categories include open-source browsers emphasizing customization and privacy, and "mindful browsers" designed to enhance user well-being. This signals a significant paradigm shift in how users interact with the internet.

> The latest browser wars are fundamentally about which company's AI will act on your behalf within the browser itself.

## Anthropic Wants to Develop Its Own Drugs
![STKB364_CLAUDE_2_A_3800fc (1)](https://platform.theverge.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/07/STKB364_CLAUDE_2_A_3800fc-1.jpg?quality=90&strip=all&crop=0%2C0%2C100%2C100&w=2400)
**Anthropic**, a major player in the AI industry known for its **Claude** models and coding tools, is now making a bold move into drug development. At their "The Briefing: AI for Science" event, the company unveiled **Claude Science**, an "AI workbench for scientists" designed to integrate fragmented tools and datasets, and generate figures and visuals. This initiative is framed around AI's potential to significantly accelerate scientific discovery and healthcare interventions, with a long list of biotech and pharma customers already utilizing **Claude**.

Going a step further, **Anthropic's** Head of Life Sciences, **Eric Kauderer-Abrams**, announced the company's intention to develop its own drugs, specifically focusing on treatments for "neglected" diseases. This positions **Anthropic** uniquely among frontier AI companies, as it will be both selling AI software to drugmakers and potentially competing with them. While other tech giants like **OpenAI**, **Amazon**, and **Google** also offer life sciences tools, **Anthropic's** direct foray into drug discovery represents one of the most significant such commitments. Specific details about their drug development plans, however, remain scarce.

> Anthropic is venturing directly into drug development, aiming to discover treatments for "neglected" diseases, while also selling AI tools to other pharmaceutical companies.

## Agentic AI Architecture
![Agentic AI Architecture - infoq.com](https://imgopt.infoq.com/fit-in/375x480/filters:quality(80))
As AI systems become increasingly sophisticated and autonomous, understanding the architecture behind "Agentic AI" is becoming paramount for professional software development. This concept refers to AI systems capable of perceiving their environment, making decisions, and taking actions to achieve specific goals, often without constant human oversight. The evolving landscape of AI demands that developers grasp how these agentic systems are designed, built, and integrated into broader software ecosystems.

**InfoQ.com** highlights the critical need for knowledge in this area, offering resources like webinars and certifications focused on AI Engineering and AI Security & Privacy. These programs emphasize key components such as retrieval, agents, evaluations, and robust infrastructure, particularly in high-stakes applications like incident response. The architectural principles of agentic AI are central to developing production-ready AI systems that are both effective and governable, ensuring security, privacy, and reliable performance.

> Professional software development must increasingly focus on understanding and implementing Agentic AI Architecture to build autonomous, goal-oriented AI systems effectively and securely.
