## Scientists Use AI and Quantum Computing for Faster Drug Discovery
![Scientists’ Side Hustle? Using AI and Quantum Computing to Generate New Peptides - WIRED](https://media.wired.com/photos/6a50c6b5d12b964679cc91a9/191:100/w_1280,c_limit/Quantum%20Computing.jpg)
In a testament to ingenuity, a team at the **Technical University of Denmark (DTU)**, led by professor **Timothy Patrick Jenkins**, has successfully demonstrated how **quantum computing** can enhance generative **AI models** for drug discovery. Operating on a tight budget and during weekends, these researchers collaborated with British startup **ORCA Computing**, utilizing a printer-sized quantum computer to speed up their **AI** processes. This hybrid approach generated novel **peptides**—short chains of amino acids crucial for vaccine development—with greater accuracy than traditional methods, especially where training data was sparse.

This pioneering work, driven by a belief that "most innovative science is too scary for foundations," holds immense promise for personalized immunotherapies and vaccines. The team's model produced more successful **peptides** in laboratory tests, confirming the practical application of their quantum-enhanced **AI**. **Patrick Jenkins**, initially a "huge quantum skeptic," now sees the technology as a key to addressing the lack of diverse genetic data in medical research, which has historically focused on Western populations.

> The strongest improvements from the hybrid **AI** and **quantum computing** model were observed where training data was rare, indicating its potential to address understudied groups and rare diseases.

## Reed Jobs Forges Ahead in AI-Powered Cancer Research with Yosemite
![SAN FRANCISCO, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: Yosemite Investor Reed Jobs speaks onstage during TechCrunch Disrupt 2023 at Moscone Center on September 19, 2023 in San Francisco, California. (Photo by Kimberly White/Getty Images for TechCrunch)](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/53200091183_f376523593_k.jpg?w=680)
**Reed Jobs**, son of **Steve Jobs**, is making significant strides in the biotech world with his oncology-focused venture firm, **Yosemite**, launched in **2023**. His primary ambition is to cure cancer, leveraging a unique blend of philanthropy and traditional venture capital to build biotech companies from early academic research. **Jobs** is particularly enthusiastic about the accelerating opportunities presented by the convergence of **AI** with drug discovery and clinical trial design.

**Yosemite's** portfolio already includes promising ventures like **Azalea**, which originated from a grant to **Jennifer Doudna’s** lab and is now in clinical trials, and **Quarry**. The latter, co-founded with **Craig Crews**, explores a novel therapeutic approach called induced proximity, where drugs guide disease-causing proteins to the cell’s natural breakdown systems. **Jobs** remains committed to expanding **Yosemite's** influence, driven by both competitive spirit and the rapid growth of the opportunity space in **AI-powered oncology**.

> **Reed Jobs**'s **Yosemite** is rapidly expanding its impact by integrating **AI** into drug discovery and clinical trial design, accelerating the fight against cancer.

## OpenAI Shifts Focus to Families as ChatGPT Penetrates Households
![OpenAI ChatGPT website displayed on a laptop screen is seen in this illustration photo.](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/GettyImages-2170386424.jpg?w=1024)
Over **three years** since its launch brought generative **AI** into the mainstream, **OpenAI** is expanding its strategy beyond individual users to embrace families. The company is actively recruiting a dedicated product manager in **San Francisco** to develop experiences specifically for "families, caregivers, and older adults" across its product line, according to a recent job posting. This move signals a significant shift towards integrating **ChatGPT** and other **AI** tools more deeply into household life.

This strategic pivot aligns with changing demographics in **ChatGPT's** user base. **Sensor Tower** estimates reveal that globally, the share of users aged **35 and older** increased to **31%** in **Q2** from **26%** a year prior, while users aged **18 to 24** saw a decrease from **34%** to **29%**. In the U.S., nearly **one in four** smartphone users who are parents accessed **ChatGPT** during the quarter, a rise from **16%** a year earlier. This indicates that **OpenAI** is following a path similar to that of **Google**, **Apple**, and **Meta**, as its platform becomes increasingly embedded in daily life.

> **OpenAI's** new product manager role for families underscores a strategic move to position **ChatGPT** as a household technology, mirroring the evolution of major tech platforms.

## Meta Removes Controversial AI Feature on Instagram After Backlash
![Instagram icon](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/instagram-header.jpg?w=1024)
**Meta** has swiftly rolled back a controversial **AI feature** on **Instagram** that permitted users to modify photos from public accounts using **AI**. The feature, which was part of a broader release of **AI tools** earlier this week, was deemed to have "missed the mark" by the company and is no longer available. This decision came after immediate and widespread backlash from users concerned about privacy and control over their images.

The problematic feature allowed individuals to generate new images by @-mentioning public **Instagram** accounts, without notifying the original photo owner that their content was being used this way. **TechCrunch** even published a guide on how users could disable the feature. Following the outcry, **Meta** announced its removal via a blog post, with **Puck News** founding partner **Dylan Byers** being the first to report the company's reversal. **Meta** stated its original intent was to provide a creative tool, but acknowledged the feature's failure in execution regarding user control.

> **Meta's** rapid removal of a controversial **AI feature** on **Instagram** underscores the critical importance of user consent and privacy in the deployment of new **AI** capabilities.
