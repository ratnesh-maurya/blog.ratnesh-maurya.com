## Meta quietly launches vibe-coded gaming app Pocket

![The Meta Platforms, Inc. logo is seen on a smartphone screen.](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Meta-image.jpg?w=1024)

**Meta** has made a quiet but significant move into the gaming sector with the introduction of **Pocket**, a new application designed to enable users to create and share small, interactive games and apps using AI prompts. This initiative stems from **Meta's** earlier acquisition of the team behind the **vibe-coded gaming platform Gizmo**. The **Pocket** app facilitates the generation of these interactive experiences, referred to as "gizmos," and also features a scrollable feed where users can discover and play creations from others.

Initial observations from app intelligence provider **Appfigures** indicate that **Pocket** was first launched on **Google Play** earlier, with reverse engineer **Alessandro Paluzzi** noting its public appearance on **X** today. Screenshots from the **Google Play Store** highlight similarities between **Pocket** and the original **Gizmo** app, which remains available, both offering AI-driven content creation and discovery feeds.

> **Meta's Pocket** represents a subtle but strategic entry into AI-powered interactive content generation, leveraging the growing trend of user-created, 'vibe-coded' gaming experiences.

## OpenAI proposed donating 5% of its equity to a US sovereign wealth fund

![Donald Trump and Sam Altman](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GettyImages-2194584986.jpg?w=1024)

**OpenAI** CEO **Sam Altman** has put forward a proposal to donate **5%** of the company’s equity to a U.S. sovereign wealth fund, according to reports from the **Financial Times**. This suggestion is reportedly aimed at securing positive relations with the current administration and addressing potential political criticisms. The proposal suggests that other AI companies might follow suit with similar equity donations.

Discussions around a public AI fund have been ongoing, with previous reports from **CNBC** in June and confirmations from President **Trump** indicating interest in concepts where the American public could become a partner with AI companies. While a specific equity size was not previously disclosed, this **5%** figure provides a concrete detail. The talks are still in preliminary stages, and any formal implementation would likely necessitate congressional approval, adding a layer of complexity to the process.

> **OpenAI's** offer of **5%** equity to a U.S. sovereign wealth fund signals a strategic move to align with government interests and navigate the political landscape surrounding advanced AI development.

## Z.ai launches ZCode to challenge Cursor, Claude Code and GitHub Copilot in AI coding

![Nuneybits Vector art of red code waves over Beijing skyline and ecae69a0-2b9d-4481-a6c0-311a70f0c622](https://venturebeat.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimages.ctfassets.net%2Fjdtwqhzvc2n1%2F2XcqOZt08SkY2BArbdXXU8%2F4b8912a5d1247db567241b62e9635e8b%2FNuneybits_Vector_art_of_red_code_waves_over_Beijing_skyline_and_ecae69a0-2b9d-4481-a6c0-311a70f0c622.webp%3Fw%3D1000%26q%3D100&w=3840&q=85)

**Z.ai**, formerly known as **Zhipu AI**, has officially launched **ZCode**, a new free desktop application positioned as an "Agentic Development Environment." This move marks the Beijing-based AI lab's most aggressive foray into the competitive AI-powered coding tool market, directly challenging established players like **Cursor**, **Claude Code**, **GitHub Copilot**, and **Google's Antigravity**.

**ZCode** is built around **Z.ai's** flagship **GLM-5.2** large language model and differentiates itself by focusing on long-horizon tasks. Unlike traditional IDEs that add AI features via chat or autocomplete, **ZCode** is designed for an agent-first approach, where a user describes an outcome, and the agent plans, edits, runs checks, and iterates until the goal is achieved. The environment supports continuous follow-up across multiple devices, including desktop, mobile remote, and **Feishu**/**WeChat Bot**.

> **ZCode's** agent-first design, deeply integrated with **GLM-5.2** and supporting continuous multi-device workflow, positions it as a significant challenger in the rapidly maturing **$10 billion** AI coding agent market.

## Proactive family AI agents are coming for family life — Nori’s SuperNori is one of the first

![Proactive family AI agents are coming for family life — Nori’s SuperNori is one of the first - TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/hero-photo.png?w=1024)

The smart home industry is witnessing a significant shift, moving beyond mere device control to proactive AI assistance, and **Nori's SuperNori** is leading this charge. For years, smart home innovations primarily focused on improving user interfaces for controlling connected devices. However, with the advent of large language models, expectations are evolving, and users are now seeking systems that can understand intent and autonomously complete tasks.

**SuperNori** is designed to act as a "family AI agent," addressing the complex, overlapping decisions inherent in family life—from managing schedules and groceries to attending to children's needs and last-minute plan changes. Unlike many AI products targeted at technical users or professionals, **Nori** aims to bring sophisticated AI capabilities into the messy, dynamic environment of the home, reducing the cognitive load on family members.

> **Nori's SuperNori** heralds a new era for smart homes, transforming them from device-centric to proactive, intent-driven environments managed by intelligent family AI agents.

## Neocloud Together AI raises $800M, leaps to $8.3B valuation

![Together AI founders Percy Liang, CEO Vipul Ved Prakash, CTO Ce Zhang](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Together-AI-founders-Percy-Liang-CEO-Vipul-Ved-Prakash-CTO-Ce-Zhang.jpg?w=1024)

**Together AI**, an AI neocloud company founded in **2022**, has announced a substantial **$800 million** Series C funding round, which propels its valuation to an impressive **$8.3 billion**. This latest funding round was led by **Aramco Ventures**, with key participation from a diverse group of investors including **Vista Equity Partners**, **General Catalyst**, **Emergence Capital**, **Nvidia**, **March Capital**, **Pegatron**, and **SentinelOne’s S Ventures**.

This funding marks a significant growth trajectory for **Together AI**, which previously secured a **$305 million** Series B round at a **$3.3 billion** valuation approximately **16 months** prior. The company also started strongly with a **$102.5 million** Series A round in **2023**, co-led by **Kleiner Perkins** and **Nvidia**. This latest capital injection underscores the intense investor confidence in **Together AI's** mission to make frontier AI accessible.

> **Together AI's** massive **$800 million** Series C funding round, elevating its valuation to **$8.3 billion**, highlights robust investor confidence in its AI neocloud platform and its aggressive growth in the AI accessibility market.
