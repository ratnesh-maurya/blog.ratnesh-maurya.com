## OpenAI Deepens Commitment with Commercial Launch in Brazil

![Expanding OpenAI's presence in Brazil](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/4rlOllC9pF99idP8iRM2tt/45f42b50ea2f4e04d863beb898f1a629/Sam-Altman-video.png?w=3840&q=90&fm=webp)

**OpenAI** has announced the launch of its commercial operations in Brazil, signifying a long-term commitment to the country. A local team, based in São Paulo, will collaborate with Brazilian businesses, developers, researchers, and public institutions. The goal is to leverage Brazil's rapid AI adoption for economic growth and meaningful progress within the nation.

Brazil stands out as one of **ChatGPT's** three largest markets globally by weekly active users. The number of users in the country has nearly doubled over the past year, with Brazilians now sending approximately **215 million** messages to **ChatGPT** daily. This significant engagement underscores Brazil's importance in **OpenAI's** global strategy.

## OpenAI's Vision for AI Agents: A Double-Edged Sword of Control?

![OpenAI is building AI agents for everything. Will everyone ...](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Screenshot-2026-08-23-at-8.41.55-PM.png?w=729)

**OpenAI** is reportedly developing advanced AI agents designed to take significant control over users' digital lives. These agents could manage various platforms, including email inboxes, **Slack** accounts, phones, and applications like **Notion** and **Figma**. This move raises questions about the extent of control users are willing to cede to Large Language Models (LLMs).

Andrew Ambrosino, lead engineer for **OpenAI’s** desktop app, is already testing this future by granting his app access to his personal digital ecosystem. While acknowledging potential risks, such as an AI pulling private information from DMs, Ambrosino stated, “I’ll do it for the job. I will take the personal hit.” This highlights the tension between maximizing AI utility and maintaining personal privacy and control.

> How much control are you willing to give an LLM over your digital life?

## OpenAI's Core Experimentation Team Seeks Full-Stack Engineer

![Software Engineer, Full-Stack - Core Experimentation | OpenAI](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/6iFRMxhUWyxFR6zpThf2Am/1f826b5095ca8854064989ecec465049/SEO_Banner_2400x1350_04.png?w=1600&h=900&fit=fill)

**OpenAI's** "Statsig" team, based in Bellevue, is actively seeking a Full-Stack Software Engineer for its Core Experimentation unit. This team is responsible for building and maintaining the experimentation, feature rollout, dynamic configuration, and analytics systems that enable **OpenAI** to ship products with speed, safety, and evidence. The work of this team is critical for how product, engineering, research, and go-to-market teams learn from real-world usage and make high-confidence decisions.

The Statsig team originated as an independent company focused on trustworthy experimentation and feature management before joining **OpenAI**. Now, it serves as the experimentation and rollout platform for a wide range of **OpenAI** products, including **ChatGPT**, **Codex**, model measurement, consumer experiences like ads, business subscriptions, and developer products. The role involves working across frontend, backend, SDK, data, and infrastructure surfaces to simplify complex workflows for product teams.

> This role is not only about adding features to a platform. It is about shaping the operating system OpenAI uses to decide what to launch, how to learn from it, and how to protect users and products as we scale.

The move towards open-source for corporate AI signifies a growing trend in the industry to offer more transparent and adaptable AI tools.

**Cloudflare OS** is positioned to enable enterprises to integrate AI capabilities into their operations, offering a framework that can be tailored to specific business needs. The emphasis on security and privacy within the platform suggests a focus on addressing key concerns for businesses adopting AI technologies. This development could pave the way for broader AI adoption in corporate environments, providing a flexible and secure foundation for innovation.
