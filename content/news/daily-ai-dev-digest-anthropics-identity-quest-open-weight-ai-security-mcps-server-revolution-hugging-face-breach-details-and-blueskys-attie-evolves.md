## What really happened in the Hugging Face breach
![What really happened in the Hugging Face breach - The New Stack](https://cdn.thenewstack.io/media/2026/07/d730a634-dasha-yukhymyuk-s4qtcuodkq4-unsplash-scaled.jpg)

Details have emerged regarding the **Hugging Face** breach, shedding light on the nature of the cyberattack. The incident involved an exploit targeting the platform's sandbox environment, specifically designed to interact with **OpenAI** integration tokens. It appears that the attackers leveraged vulnerabilities within this sandbox to gain unauthorized access, compromising user data and sensitive information.

This breach highlights the complex security challenges faced by AI platforms that integrate multiple services and frameworks. The focus on **OpenAI** integration tokens suggests a sophisticated attack vector aimed at exploiting inter-service dependencies. Understanding the specifics of this incident is crucial for developers and security professionals to fortify their own environments against similar threats in the future.

> The **Hugging Face** breach specifically targeted **OpenAI** integration tokens within the platform's sandbox environment.

## Bluesky’s AI assistant Attie expands into an open social research tool
![Bluesky’s AI assistant Attie expands into an open social research tool](https://techcrunch.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/attie-logo.webp?w=1200)

**Bluesky's** AI assistant, **Attie**, initially launched in **March**, is evolving beyond its role in building custom social feeds without programming. The company has announced a significant expansion, transforming **Attie** into an open-ended social research tool. This new capability, dubbed **Quests**, allows users to query **Attie** for information and trending news across the broader **Bluesky** social network, known as the Atmosphere, encompassing all apps integrating with the underlying **AT Protocol**.

This strategic move by **Bluesky** comes as the social network seeks to diversify its offerings and potentially monetize its platform, especially after seeing its user growth slow. While its user base doubled to **40 million** by **October** of last year, it currently stands at approximately **45.6 million registered accounts**. The expansion of **Attie** into a research tool is an effort to enhance user engagement and provide more value, potentially attracting new users and retaining existing ones by offering advanced analytical capabilities for social discourse.

> **Bluesky's AI assistant Attie** now includes a **Quests** feature, transforming it into an open social research tool for the entire **AT Protocol** ecosystem.
