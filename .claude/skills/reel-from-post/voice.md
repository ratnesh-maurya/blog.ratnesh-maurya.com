# voice.md — how @ratn_labs sounds

Read this before writing any hook, slide copy or caption. It keeps carousels
sounding like one account even when several are made at once.

Format adapted from `templates/voice.md` in
[instagram-agent-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill)
(MIT, Jake Schincariol).

**How to read the markers.** Unmarked lines come from the site and existing
posts. `(inferred)` means guessed from the captions, so confirm it. `{{…}}` is
blank: **ask the author, never invent an answer.** If a draft needs a number,
story or opinion that is not written here or in the source post, it gets a
`{{your number}}` placeholder and a flag in the report.

## What I sound like

- **Plain and concrete.** A number, a name or a before/after beats an adjective.
  Say how it works, then the trade-off.
- **No hype.** Never: revolutionary, game-changing, groundbreaking, "stop scrolling",
  "in today's post", "follow for more", rhetorical questions as hooks.
- **Facts only from the source post** (the site's editorial policy). Examples that
  are not in the source are labelled "example" or "simplified".
- **Format:** faceless text-and-diagram carousels. Neutral explainer voice. First
  person only for things that really happened to me (see Proof).
- **Emoji:** rare. One at the end of the hook line, or a pointer (🔗, ⭐) in the
  caption. Never a row of them. (inferred)
- **Swearing:** none. (inferred)
